# Stardust Shift

*Dodge, blast and collect stardust across three galaxies, then upgrade your ship to take on the bosses waiting at the end of each one.*

A lane-based 2D space shooter built in Unity for WebGL.

**Publisher:** Cyan Circuit Games
**Developer & Designer:** Zacahry Ziegler

**Play it:** (https://bluerobotminer.itch.io/stardustshift)

## About

Pilot your ship through asteroid fields, dodge turret fire and blast your way through three galaxies. Break asteroids to collect Stardust, grab supply crates to repair your hull or recharge your shield, and push through the waves until a boss blocks the way. Defeat it, ride the portal to the next galaxy, and spend your Stardust on permanent upgrades.

## Features

- 3 levels, each ending in a boss fight that gets tougher every time
- 4 upgrade paths with 24 levels each: Hull, Shield, Firepower and Stardust
- Shiny and Rainbow asteroids that drop better loot
- Supply crates that repair your hull or recharge your shield
- Homing torpedoes that lock onto targets in front of you
- Progress and settings save automatically in the browser

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Change lane | W / S or Up / Down |
| Speed boost | Hold D or Right |
| Return to normal speed | A or Left |
| Fire lasers | Space or Left Click |
| Launch torpedo | E or Right Click |
| Pause | Esc |

## Settings

Music volume, SFX volume, fullscreen, quality (Low/High), screen shake and auto-fire.

## Built With

- Unity 2022.1.7f1
- Universal Render Pipeline (2D)
- TextMesh Pro

## Building

1. Open the project in Unity 2022.1.7f1.
2. Go to **File > Build Settings**, select **WebGL** and make sure these scenes are listed in order: `MainMenu`, `Level 1`, `Level 2`, `Level 3`, `LevelComplete`.
3. Click **Build**.
